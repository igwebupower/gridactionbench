# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GridActionBench is an open research project. We want participation — code, scenarios, data, documentation, and review — to be accessible to energy-domain experts, AI researchers, and software engineers alike, regardless of experience level, background, or institutional affiliation.

## Expected behaviour

- Engage in good faith. Disagreement about benchmark design, scenario validity, or evaluator correctness is expected and welcome — GridActionBench's own methodology (`docs/benchmark/METHODOLOGY.md` §8) explicitly asks reviewers to find what's wrong, not to be agreeable.
- Back claims with evidence where possible, especially for energy-domain assertions (physical plausibility, GB-specific mechanisms) and benchmark-methodology assertions (construct validity, statistical power).
- Be respectful of differing expertise — a domain reviewer without software engineering background and a contributor without power-systems background are both valuable, and neither should be dismissed for gaps outside their area.
- Focus criticism on the work (a scenario, an evaluator, a claim, a pull request), not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or conduct.
- Deliberately submitting misleading data provenance, fabricated review credentials, or falsified benchmark results (see also `docs/benchmark/CONTAMINATION_POLICY.md` and the research-integrity rules in `docs/project/PID.md`-adjacent governance).
- Sustained bad-faith disruption of issues, pull requests, or review discussions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), and any official project communication channels established later.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behaviour should be directed to the project maintainers (see `GOVERNANCE.md` for current maintainer contacts). Maintainers will review and respond to reports as promptly as reasonably possible, and may take any action appropriate to the situation, including warnings, temporary or permanent bans from project spaces, or rejection of contributions.

## Attribution

This document is a project-specific statement of expected conduct rather than a verbatim adoption of an external template; projects seeking a more extensive standard reference may also wish to consult the Contributor Covenant.
